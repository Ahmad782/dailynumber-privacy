In short: Daily Number ("the app") is published on Google Play by LALU KHAN ("we", "us", "the developer"). The app works entirely offline. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no server.

Who this policy covers

This privacy policy applies to the Android app Daily Number (package name com.mikao.dailynumber), distributed on Google Play under the developer name LALU KHAN. LALU KHAN is the developer responsible for the app and for this policy.

Information we collect

None. Daily Number does not collect any personal or sensitive user data. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, files or device identifiers, and the app has no way to send data to us or to anyone else.

Data stored on your device

To show your recent history, the app saves the last five numbers it generated in the app's private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's data in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions and internet access

Daily Number requests no Android permissions and does not use the internet. All features work without a connection.

Third-party services

The app contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and no other third-party services that receive user data. Nothing is shared with third parties because nothing is collected.

Data retention and deletion

We hold no user data, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. The recent-history list stored on your device stays there until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Security

The recent-history list is kept in the app's private storage, which Android protects from other apps. Because no data is transmitted, no personal data is stored on external servers.

Children's privacy

Daily Number does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. Since Daily Number collects no personal data, we hold nothing about you. If you have any questions, you can still contact us using the details below.
